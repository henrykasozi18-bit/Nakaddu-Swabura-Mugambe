Admissions

Everything you need to know about applying to St. Lawrence University

Application Process

Choose Your Program

Browse our academic programs and select the one that aligns with your career goals. Review the entry requirements for your chosen program to ensure you meet the criteria.

Obtain an Application Form

Download the application form from our website or collect a physical copy from our admissions office. Complete all sections of the form accurately and thoroughly.

Prepare Required Documents

Gather the necessary documents including certified copies of your certificates, result slips, national ID or passport, and recent passport-size photographs.

Submit Your Application

Submit your completed application form together with all required documents and the application fee to the admissions office or through our online portal.

Receive Admission Letter

Once your application is reviewed and approved, you will receive an admission letter with instructions on registration, fee payment, and reporting dates.

Register and Begin Your Journey

Complete your registration by paying the required fees, collecting your student ID, and attending the orientation program. Welcome to St. Lawrence University!

Entry Requirements

Undergraduate Programs

Prerequisites:

• UACE (A-Level) with at least 2 principal passes or equivalent

• U-Certificate (O-Level) with at least 6 credits including Mathematics and English

• Relevant Diploma with credit or better from a recognized institution

• Minimum age of 17 years

Postgraduate Programs

Prerequisites:

• Relevant Bachelor's degree with second class honor or better

• Minimum of 2 years work experience for professional programs

• Letter of recommendation and statement of purpose

• Interview may be required for some programs

Diploma Programs

Prerequisites:

• U-Certificate (O-Level) with at least 5 credits

• Credit in English and Mathematics

• Minimum age of 16 years

• Relevant work experience may be considered

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Merit-Based Scholarships

Outstanding academic performers may qualify for partial or full tuition waivers based on their academic records and performance in entrance assessments.

Need-Based Financial Aid

Students from financially disadvantaged backgrounds may apply for need-based bursaries and sponsorships to support their education.

Sponsorship Programs

We partner with organizations and government programs that offer sponsorship opportunities for eligible students in specific fields of study.
