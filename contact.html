How to Get to Campus

By Road

Our campus is located along Kira Road, easily accessible from central Kampala. Major bus and taxi routes pass by the campus, making commute convenient.

By Public Transport

Take any bus or taxi heading along Kira Road from Kampala city center (Nasser Road or Kabalagala) and alight at the St. Lawrence University stop.

From Entebbe Airport

Entebbe International Airport is approximately 40km from our campus. Taxis and airport shuttle services are available for the journey to Kampala.
